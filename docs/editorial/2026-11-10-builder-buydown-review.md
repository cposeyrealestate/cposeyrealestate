## Official factual sources opened November 10

- Fannie Mae B2-1.4-04, Temporary Interest Rate Buydowns, dated August 7, 2024: https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b2-1.4-04/temporary-interest-rate-buydowns . Written subsidy agreements, unchanged note terms, qualification at note rate for these loans, applicable interested-party contribution limits, and agreement-dependent disposition of unused funds. Do not generalize program-specific rules to every mortgage.
- Fannie Mae Loan Delivery job aid: https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/job-aid/loan-delivery/topic/overview_of_temp_buydown.htm . A temporary subsidy reduces the borrower's early outlay; the actual mortgage note rate and contractual payment are not reduced. Any refresh should correct the existing article's loose wording that says a temporary buydown lowers the note rate.
- CFPB points and lender credits explainer, reviewed October 19, 2023: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-should-i-use-lender-credits-and-points-also-called-discount-points-en-136/ . Points exchange upfront cost for a lower rate; evaluate several holding periods, not a universal best choice. Do not promise one point buys a fixed rate reduction.
- CFPB Compare and negotiate your loan offers: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/compare/compare-loan-estimates/ . Compare loan structure, rate, principal/interest, mortgage insurance, escrow, upfront loan costs, lender credits and cash to close; dates matter because rates change.
